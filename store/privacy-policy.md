# Privacy policy — Traction

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Traction is a Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page with a board of startup milestones posted by its users.

## What is stored on your own device

The following never leaves your browser:

- The cached milestone board, so a new tab opens without a network request
- Milestones you have saved
- Milestones you have cheered
- Your own posts while they are awaiting review
- The display name you last used
- A randomly generated identifier, created in your browser, used only to enforce the posting limit

None of this is uploaded, and none of it is readable by anyone but you.

## What is sent, and only when you choose to post

When you submit a milestone, this is sent and stored:

- The milestone type, the name of what you are building, the optional number, your one-line note, and the optional link
- Anything you choose to add to your startup profile: a description, the stage you are at, what you are open to, a website, a social link, and a link to your own LinkedIn profile. All of these are optional, and all are published publicly on the board
- The display name you typed, if you typed one
- The randomly generated identifier described above, used to enforce the two-posts-per-day limit
- A second randomly generated token that stays in your browser and is what lets you edit or delete your own post later

When you cheer a milestone, only the identifier of that milestone and your random identifier are sent.

Nothing is sent unless you press a button that sends it.

## What is never collected

- Your name, email address, or any account
- Your browsing history, the pages you visit, or the contents of any page
- Your IP address is not logged or retained by us beyond the transient handling of the request
- Location, analytics, tracking, cookies, and advertising identifiers: none are used

There is no login. There is no account.

## Where it is stored

Milestone posts are stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted in the European Union. Approved posts are published as a public JSON file distributed via the jsDelivr CDN. Anything you post is intended to be seen publicly by other users of the extension.

## Review before publication

Every submitted milestone is reviewed before it appears on the public board. Posts may be rejected without notice.

## Editing or removing something you posted

You can edit or delete your own posts from the extension, on the browser you posted from. Deleting removes the record from the database straight away; because the public board is rebuilt on a schedule, it can take up to an hour to disappear from other people's boards. Editing sends the post back for review, so it leaves the public board until it is approved again.

If you posted from a browser you no longer have, email the address below with the name of the startup and roughly when you posted. Because there is no account, that is what identifies the post.

## Children

Traction is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
